Restoring

The commands above print what they would write and stop. Adding --yes does it, and is a second deliberate act. Nothing is ever deleted by a restore: a document in the archive is written over whatever is there now, and anything the archive does not have is left exactly as it is. 

node scripts/backup/restore.mjs firestore <collection> --yes
