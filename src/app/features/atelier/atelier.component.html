The engine is not answering

This page is a front end for a program running on this computer, and that program is not reachable at 127.0.0.1:7860. There is nothing wrong with the site.

Three things do this, and from here they look the same:

  • It is not running. Start it from atelier-engine/: .venv\Scripts\python -m uvicorn engine.service:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 7860
  • You are on a different machine. The models and the card are on the one the engine runs on. This page cannot reach across to them, and is not meant to.
  • Chrome refused the local connection. A page served from the internet needs permission to reach an address on your own machine. Look for the prompt, or the icon at the left of the address bar.

Switching it on asks Windows to run it, through a handler registered once from atelier-engine/tools/atelier-protocol.reg. Without that the switch has nothing to ask, and the engine has to be started from a terminal.
